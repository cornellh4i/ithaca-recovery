# Switch Between Light and Dark Mode

The calendar, admin pages, and Resources all come in a light and a dark theme. You don't need
to be signed in to choose one.

There are three settings:

- **Light**: always the light theme.
- **Dark**: always the dark theme.
- **System** (the default): matches your computer's or phone's own light/dark setting, and
  switches automatically when that setting changes (for example, if your phone goes dark at
  sunset).

## On a computer

Click the theme button in the top-right of the navigation bar, just left of your profile picture
(or the **Sign In** button). Each click moves to the next setting: **Light → Dark → System**.
Hover over the button to see which setting is on now.

- ☀ (sun): Light
- ☾ (moon): Dark
- **A** in a circle: System

## On a phone

Tap **☰** (top-left) to open the menu. The **Theme** row at the bottom has **Light**, **Dark**,
and **System**; tap the one you want.

## Good to know

- Your choice is remembered on that device and browser only. Your phone and laptop each keep
  their own setting, and clearing your browser's site data resets it to **System**.
- Room colors don't change between themes. A meeting in the Serenity Room is the same green
  either way, so you can still find meetings by color.
- Printing always uses the light theme, whatever setting you have on.
- The **/signage** display board always uses the light theme.
